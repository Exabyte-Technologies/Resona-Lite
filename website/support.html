Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about Resona Lite.

Does Resona Lite require an internet connection?

No. Resona Lite is designed for offline use. You can enjoy its locally generated ambient music and soundscapes without an active internet connection.

How do I use the sleep timer?

While listening, open the sleep timer controls and choose your preferred duration. Resona Lite will automatically stop playback when the timer finishes.

Can I listen while my screen is locked?

Resona Lite supports background audio playback on compatible devices. If playback stops unexpectedly, try reopening the app and restarting your listening session.

What should I do if no sound is playing?

Check your device's volume and audio output, including connected headphones or Bluetooth devices. Try pausing and restarting playback. If the issue continues, contact support with your device model and app version.

How can I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Send an email to support@exabyte.org.cn with the subject "Resona Lite Feedback". For bug reports, include the steps to reproduce the problem and your device model.

Is Resona Lite the same as Resona?

No. Resona Lite is a separate, simplified application designed for offline listening. This support page is specifically for Resona Lite, not the full Resona application.
